Chennai IT Companies

Chennai is a growing hub for IT services, with companies excelling in software development, SaaS, and consulting.Chennai is India's second-largest IT exporter, featuring over 1,500 tech firms and a booming SaaS ecosystem. Major IT corridors like Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR) and Guindy house the city's top technology parks (e.g., Tidel Park, Ramanujan IT City).

Bangalore IT Companies

Bangalore, known as the Silicon Valley of India, hosts global tech giants and innovative startups.Bangalore is the IT hub of India. Top global and domestic IT companies are headquartered or have large delivery centers in the city. Major locations include Electronic City, Whitefield, and Manyata Tech Park. Freshers usually start with salaries ranging from ₹3.5 Lakhs Per Annum (LPA) to ₹15 LPA, depending on the firm.

Hyderabad IT Companies

Hyderabad is a major IT destination, with a strong presence of multinational corporations and consulting firms.Hyderabad is a major IT and Global Capability Center (GCC) hub in India. It houses over 1,500 established software companies. Leading tech giants include Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Accenture, and TCS. These firms are located in primary tech districts like Hitec City, Gachibowli, and Madhapur.
